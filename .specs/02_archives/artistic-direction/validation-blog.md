# Validation: `v3/Blog — 1920 — Dark` (`2116:869`)

Task 6 of the Figma design-system cleanup plan (file `ihWIWmvtQPTWgUxlrVjC2c`). Validates
that the Phase 1–4 cleanup (dashed-border removal, radius collapse to `{9999, 8, 0}`, button
vocabulary closure, dead-component deletion) reads correctly on the real Blog page frame, and
checks the Blog-specific component contracts (`SerieCard`, `PostRow`, `ArchiveTable`, topic chips).

Method: full-frame screenshot at `scale: 0.28`, section-by-section screenshots at `scale: 0.6`
(Header `2116:870`, BlogTextContainer `2119:7432`, BlogSeriesSection `2121:7669`, PostListSection
`2125:8171`, Footer `2116:876` — the actual children of `PageContent (slot)` `2119:7421` /
`PageContentContainer` `2119:7425`), plus a programmatic subtree scan of the entire `2116:869`
frame (`findAll`/`query` + property inspection) for dashed strokes, non-standard corner radii,
drop-shadow effects, gradient fills, font-family usage, and every use of the accent color
(bound `VariableID:2328:2` and its raw teal value, to catch unbound leaks too). Geometry checks
(`absoluteBoundingBox`) were used to confirm the `SerieCard` cover inset, and a side-by-side
property diff (fills/strokes/text) was run between the `PostRow` `State=default` and
`State=hover` component-set variants. No drift found — **all 9 base checklist items and all 5
Blog-specific items pass**. No source components were fixed.
